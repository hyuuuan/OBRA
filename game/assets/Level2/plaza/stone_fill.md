# Plaza stone fill

Generated with the built-in image tool from the user's stone close-up. Runtime repeats this
square texture at 320 world units with nearest filtering and mirrored edges. The decorative
retaining wall keeps its original proportions; its bottom cut follows the dark mortar to
join this fill without the old straight dirt band.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a square seamless repeating game texture of ONLY the underground brown stones in the reference. Reference image is the exact style and palette target: chunky irregular squared brown stones, thick dark charcoal brown joints, sparse ochre highlights on upper left, coarse low resolution pixel-art clusters. Match it closely. About 8 stones across and 8 rows. Flat orthographic side view, even illumination across the entire texture. Fill all edges with the same material, seamless on all four sides, opaque. No grass, soil stripes, borders, labels, gradients, perspective, or transparency. This will extend an existing pixel art retaining wall downwards; preserve roughly square stone shapes.
